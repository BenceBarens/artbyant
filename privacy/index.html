Privacy policy

We value your privacy. This website collects as little data as possible and doesn't use any tracking technologies. The only cookies used are the onces placed by third parties, such as YouTube.

Cookies and Preferences

  • Cookies: We do not place tracking, marketing, or analytics cookies on your device natively. Pages with embedded YouTube players can however place cookies provided by YouTube. To minimise tracking, the embed uses the "no-cookie" YouTube domain.
  • System Preferences: Our website only reads your local system preferences (such as language/colour scheme preferences or system font settings) to display the site correctly. These settings are not stored on our servers and are never used to identify you.

Contact form

When you use our contact form, we ask for the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Your message

This information is strictly used to read and respond to your inquiry. We will never use your email address for newsletters or marketing, nor will we ever share your details with third parties.

Server logs

When you visit our website, the web host (GitHub Pages) automatically processes standard technical data (such as your IP address) to establish a connection and keep the website secure. We do not analyze this data or use it to identify individual visitors.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information provided through the contact form. Feel free to contact us via the email address listed on this site to make a request.
